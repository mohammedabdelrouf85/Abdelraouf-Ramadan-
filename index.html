An Invitation to Research Collaboration in the Fields of Agricultural Engineering, Water Management, Sustainable Development, and Climate Change.

I invite my esteemed colleagues at the National Research Centre, agricultural research centers, and faculties of agriculture at universities in Egypt, the Arab world, and internationally to engage in joint research collaboration, combining and focusing efforts on applied research projects and related studies in:

  • Agricultural Engineering and Water Management
  • Integrated Projects, Sustainable Development, and Climate Change
  • Simulation Models

Scientific integration, leveraging the capabilities of each individual, and unifying similar research projects leads to more accurate results, saves time and money, and accelerates completion and publication.
